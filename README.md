# Global E-Commerce Executive & Operations Dashboard

## Executive Summary
This **Tableau Executive & Operations Dashboard** provides a holistic view of an e-commerce business's revenue performance, profitability, regional expansion, and fulfillment logistics. It empowers stakeholders to monitor executive-level KPIs, evaluate high-performing product categories, analyze customer payment behaviors, and optimize shipping mode efficiencies[cite: 11].

## Tools & Technologies Used
* **Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public:** Geographic mapping (Mapbox integration), KPI cards, donut distribution charts, line trend visuals, bar chart comparisons[cite: 11].
* **Data Visualizations:** Category performance, operational shipping cost, regional heatmaps[cite: 11].

---

## Dashboard Visualizations & Key Features

1. **Category-wise Sales Performance:**
   * Breakdown of sales across product categories: **Electronics ($360,731)**, **Furniture ($143,680)**, **Sports ($67,711)**, **Home & Kitchen ($51,109)**, **Clothing ($27,545)**, and **Beauty ($16,398)**[cite: 11].
2. **Sales by Months (Yearly Trend):**
   * Tracks monthly sales trajectory, showing peaks in **February ($90,775)** and **April ($77,870)**[cite: 11].
3. **Geographic Regional Distribution Map:**
   * Interactive map highlighting regional sales revenue: **North ($148,153)**, **East ($117,026)**, and **Central ($103,239)**[cite: 11].
4. **Customer Payment Preferences:**
   * Distribution of payment modes: **Credit Card (30.78%)**, **UPI (22.77%)**, **Debit Card (22.48%)**, **Net Banking (10.99%)**, **Cash on Delivery (9.00%)**, and **Wallet (3.98%)**[cite: 11].
5. **Shipping Mode Cost Efficiency:**
   * Comparison of fulfillment orders/costs across **Standard Shipping (118,476)**, **Express Shipping (63,435)**, and **Same Day Shipping (22,197)**[cite: 11].

## Key Business Insights
* **Revenue Driver:** **Electronics** is by far the primary revenue generator, contributing over **54% of overall sales ($360.7K)**[cite: 11].
* **Digital Payment Dominance:** Digital payment methods (Credit Card, UPI, Debit Card) account for over **75% of total transactions**, indicating a strong shift toward digital payments[cite: 11].
* **Logistics Optimization:** **Standard Shipping** remains the most heavily utilized delivery mode, while Same-Day fulfillment forms a smaller segment[cite: 11].
